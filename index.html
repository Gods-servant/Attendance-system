STAFF WORKFLOW

How Attendance Works — 4 Simple Steps

Designed for every staff member, no training needed.

1
Arrive at Office

Staff arrives at the office during work hours. The single official QR is displayed at the entrance & reception.

2
Scan Office QR

Using any phone camera, staff scans the one main office QR. It opens the secure attendance website instantly.

3
Login Securely

Staff logs in with Staff ID + password. Identity is verified — the QR itself holds no personal info.

4
Tap Clock In / Out

One tap records date, time & status (Present/Late). Same process at close of work for Time-Out.

Attendance Rules Enforced Automatically

One Clock-In per day • Clock-Out only after Clock-In • Late if after 09:00 + 15 min grace • No double marking • Staff cannot view others' records • Sessions expire on logout.
